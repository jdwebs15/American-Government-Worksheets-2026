Part II • CS 6

Federalists vs. Anti-Federalists

Read both excerpts, then compare the competing viewpoints.

Federalist No. 51 — James Madison

“If men were angels, no government would be necessary… You must first enable the government to control the governed; and in the next place oblige it to control itself.”

Brutus I — Anti-Federalist

“This government is to possess absolute and uncontrollable power, legislative, executive, and judicial, with respect to every object to which it extends… It is as complete a system of despotism as ever was framed.”
